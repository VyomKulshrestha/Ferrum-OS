# FerrumOS Incident-Dynamics and Real-Telemetry Evidence Dataset

## Summary

This archival dataset accompanies *Prediction Is Not Permission: Cross-Domain World Models Under
Deterministic Runtime Authority*, Technical Report v1.3. It freezes two evidence lineages.

1. **Incident-dynamics studies v1-v4.** Registered prediction and warning studies in a FerrumOS
   operating-system simulator and a Physical JEPA cyber-physical simulator. Public incident
   reports (32 verified sources) motivate abstract mechanism families; the episodes themselves
   are generated by researcher-designed simulators. The package retains every protocol,
   selection, final result, verification, development ledger, retained final catalog and compact
   model archive, including the failed v1 attempt and the failed v3 primary rule.
2. **Real-telemetry studies rt1 and rt2.** Attack and incident detection on public recorded
   telemetry: HAI 22.04 (rt1), HAI 21.03 and HAI 23.05 (rt2), and the Server Machine Dataset
   (SMD, rt1). World models are trained on normal operation only. Synthetic injections built
   from 35 verified attack and failure sources (MITRE ATT&CK for ICS, CISA advisories, Symantec,
   peer-reviewed ICS and cloud-failure studies) train the incident-informed detectors.

## Upstream data are not redistributed

HAI (ETRI, CC BY-SA 4.0) and SMD (Su et al., KDD 2019, MIT) recordings are not included.
`records/real_telemetry_data_manifest_v1.json` pins the upstream repository, commit, file names,
sizes and SHA-256 digests (verified against Git LFS oids or Git blob hashes).
`scripts/fetch_real_telemetry.py` downloads and verifies them into `target/real-telemetry/raw/`.
Cite the upstream datasets when using them.

## Contents

- `records/` - protocols, amendments, selection freezes, final results, verifications, post-hoc
  analyses, source catalogs and the upstream data manifest for both lineages.
- `artifacts/incident-dynamics-v1` to `-v4` - retained development records, calibration and
  final catalogs (NPZ), open-event records and compact LightGBM or PyTorch model archives.
- `artifacts/real-telemetry-rt1`, `-rt2` - development ledgers and final-access ledgers.
- `scripts/` - generators, training, selection, final evaluation and verification code, with the
  import closure needed to run them; `requirements-prediction-report-v1_2.txt` pins the audit
  dependencies.
- `figures/` - result figures used in the report.

## Access discipline

Each study registered its protocol before final access, froze its selection with digests, and
opened its final data once. Real-telemetry test files are read only through ledgered loaders;
`artifacts/real-telemetry-*/final_access*.jsonl` records every read, including one labelled
post-hoc analysis of rt1. HAI 21.03 and HAI 23.05 test files had been used by earlier FerrumOS
studies before rt2; rt2 is a within-lineage replication on previously used public data.

## Intended uses

- Audit or reproduce the registered selections and final evaluations.
- Compare world-model forecasters and warning detectors on identical catalogs and recordings.
- Reuse the verified incident and literature source catalogs and the injection families.

## Limitations and claim boundary

- Incident-dynamics episodes, labels and perturbation ranges are researcher-authored; narratives
  supply no telemetry.
- HAI attacks are staged by the dataset authors on one testbed; SMD labels have documented
  quality issues. Real-telemetry results concern detection on recordings, not prevention.
- No runtime authority, actuator delivery, HIL, deployment, certification, formal safety proof,
  independent execution or independent assessment is involved or claimed.
- Pickled detector bundles are kept locally and are not distributed; they are regenerable from
  the registered code and seeds, and their SHA-256 digests are in the selection records.
